How to read this. The index counts open positions by direction only. A lot of Hypurr wallets aren't taking a bet at all — they run delta-neutral, hedged, or market-making books, so a "long" you see here is often just one leg against a short somewhere else (a funding-rate carry, a basis trade, an LP hedge). That's why holders can lean heavily long while the biggest dollars lean short. Read it as a live positioning snapshot of the pride — not crowd sentiment, and never a signal to copy.
