What Hypurr Terminal is

Hypurr Terminal (hypurrterminal.xyz) is an unofficial, community-built dashboard for the Hypurr NFT collection on Hyperliquid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Hyperliquid, the Hypurr team, or any related entity. It exists to visualize public information in one place.

What data it shows

Everything on this site comes from public sources: on-chain ERC-721 ownership and transfer history on HyperEVM, and open trading positions read from Hyperliquid's public API. We show direction, coin, size, notional and leverage only, never PnL or account balances. We never ask you to connect a wallet, sign a message, or create an account, and we do not read anything from your wallet.

What we collect from you

We ourselves collect nothing personal: there is no login, no account, and no advertising, and we never ask for or read your wallet. If, and only if, you accept analytics (see below), Google Analytics collects pseudonymous usage data on our behalf to help us understand traffic. We do not sell your data, and we do not combine it with your wallet or any on-chain identity.

Cookies & local storage

By default, the only thing stored in your browser is a single preference recording your choice on the cookie notice, so it does not reappear every visit. It lives in local storage, never leaves your device, and is not a tracking cookie.

If you press Accept on the cookie notice, we additionally load Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-N4VNS4KXWH). It sets first-party cookies (typically _ga and _ga_<id>) and reports pseudonymous data to Google: pages viewed, approximate region, and device/browser type, so we can see how the terminal is used. Analytics load only after consent (we use Google Consent Mode, defaulting analytics storage to denied); if you press Decline, Google Analytics is never loaded and no analytics cookies are set. Our lawful basis for analytics is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by , by clearing this site's storage, or by installing Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. Google's own handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Not financial advice

Positions shown here can be one leg of a hedge, a delta-neutral book, or a market-making inventory, not a directional bet and not a copy-trade signal. Nothing on Hypurr Terminal is financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. Do your own research.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it. Because the site keeps no contact details for visitors, we cannot notify you individually; please check back here.
